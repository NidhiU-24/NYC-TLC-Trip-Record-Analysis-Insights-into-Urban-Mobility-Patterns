# NYC TLC Trip Record Analysis Insights into Urban Mobility Patterns
As a Machine Learning Engineer on the NYC TLC Data Insights Team, your task is to analyze Green Taxi trip data to understand passenger usage patterns. The main goal is to identify key factors influencing whether drivers receive tips, enabling the development of a model to predict future tip amounts.

## Objective: 
The project analyzed TLC Trip Record Data to explore trip patterns, passenger behaviors, and service metrics for yellow and green taxis and For-Hire Vehicles (FHVs) in New York City.

## Data Preparation:

1. Cleaning and preprocessing steps were performed on large datasets.
2. Filtering was done to remove incomplete or obviously incorrect trip records (like negative fares or trip distances).
3. Trip data was integrated with location zone shapefiles to map pickups and drop-offs to NYC taxi zones.

## Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):

1. Key statistics (like average trip distance, fare amount, and passenger count) were calculated.
2. Temporal patterns were analyzed, such as trip frequency across days of the week and times of day.
3. Geospatial trends were mapped, identifying hot pickup and drop-off zones.

## Visualization:

1. Heatmaps showed trip density across different boroughs and times.
2. Bar charts and line graphs illustrated trends in payment types, tip amounts, and trip durations.

## Findings:

1. Certain zones like Midtown Manhattan and JFK Airport consistently had the highest number of pickups and drop-offs.
2. Cash payments were more frequent in green taxis compared to yellow taxis, where card payments dominated.
3. Peak trip hours were during morning and evening commute times.

## Challenges:

1. Data size was very large, requiring sampling or efficient processing techniques.
2.  Data completeness and accuracy were dependent on third-party technology providers.
